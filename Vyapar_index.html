Frequently Asked Questions

What is Vyapar software?

Vyapar is a billing and accounting software for Indian small businesses. It lets you create GST invoices, manage inventory, track customer payments, maintain business accounts, and generate GST reports from your mobile or desktop.

What is Vyapar billing software?

Vyapar billing software is the invoicing module inside Vyapar that lets you create GST-compliant invoices, estimates, and purchase orders in seconds, and share them instantly via WhatsApp or email.

How much does Vyapar cost?

Vyapar has 6 paid plans (all + GST). Basic Plan: Silver at ₹3,399/yr (Desktop) and Gold at ₹3,699/yr (Desktop). Vertical Pro: Retail Pro, Distributor Pro, and Manufacturing Pro — each at ₹8,999/yr (Desktop). Premium: Platinum at ₹9,999/yr (Desktop). Add Mobile access for ₹600 extra (Silver/Gold) or ₹1,000–₹2,000 extra (Pro/Platinum). 3-year plans save up to 35%.

Is Vyapar suitable for retailers?

Yes. Vyapar's Retail Pro plan is built for retail shops, with POS billing, weighing scale integration, an inbuilt retail item library, and customer loyalty points.

Can distributors use Vyapar?

Yes. The Distributor Pro plan supports multiple godowns, party-wise item pricing, field salesman tracking, and outstanding payment management for wholesale and distribution businesses.

Does Vyapar support GST billing?

Vyapar generates GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B reports automatically. You can export these reports and file them on the GST portal. It does not directly file returns, but makes the process very easy.

Does Vyapar work offline?

Yes, Vyapar works fully offline on Android. All data is stored locally on your device and syncs automatically when you reconnect to the internet.

Can I migrate my data to Vyapar?

Yes, Vyapar supports importing existing business data, including from Tally, so you can switch over without re-entering your records manually.

Can Vyapar import data from Tally?

Yes, Vyapar supports Tally import and export, making it easier for businesses moving from Tally to bring their existing ledgers and item data across.

How can I buy Vyapar software?

You can download Vyapar directly and purchase a plan, or talk to BizSoft Solutions for free guidance on choosing the right plan for your business type before you buy.

Is Vyapar better than Tally for small businesses?

For very small businesses and first-time software users, Vyapar is often better than Tally because it is mobile-first, easier to learn, and more affordable. Tally is more suitable for businesses with complex accounting needs or chartered accountants.
